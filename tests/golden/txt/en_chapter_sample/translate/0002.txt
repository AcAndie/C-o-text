Chapter 2: Whispers in the Brass Gear

At midnight, the cathedral bell across the river struck twelve slow notes. Arthur fitted the final glass lens into the lantern frame and turned the ignition key half a rotation clockwise.
Instead of a flame, a pale silver mist rose from the spindle, coalescing into the translucent silhouette of a harbour that had sunk beneath the northern sea two centuries ago. Ships with copper sails drifted silently across the surface of his oak desk, their rigging humming like distant violin strings.
Arthur reached for his leather ledger with a steady hand. Whatever the elders had buried in the vault beneath the foundry, this lantern was the map that would lead him straight to its door.
